12-Day Fantastic Cambodia And Vietnam Trip

This comprehensive itinerary guide presents a twelve-day cross-border tour through Cambodia and Vietnam tailored for international visitors. It outlines day-to-day sightseeing programs, transit arrangements, and accommodation details across four key regional hubs.

Featured destinations include the ancient temples of Angkor and Phnom Kulen in Siem Reap, followed by Hanoi, the karst landscapes of Trang An in Ninh Binh, and a cruise through Halong Bay. In central Vietnam, the guide covers the Golden Bridge at Ba Na Hills, the Nguyen Dynasty landmarks in Hue, and leisure time around Da Nang and Hoi An.
