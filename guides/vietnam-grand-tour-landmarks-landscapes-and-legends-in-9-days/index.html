Vietnam Grand Tour – Landmarks, Landscapes And Legends In 9 Days

This travel itinerary serves as a nine-day guide for navigating the major cultural landmarks and natural landscapes of Vietnam. It outlines scheduled transport, hotel stays, and sightseeing stops across Hanoi, Ninh Binh, Ha Long Bay, Da Nang, Hoi An, and Ho Chi Minh City.

Key locations include Hoa Lu ancient capital, the UNESCO-listed Trang An grottoes, Ha Long Bay caves, and Cam Thanh Coconut Village. Travelers also explore Hoi An Old Town on foot, ride the cable car at Ba Na Hills to see the Golden Bridge, and inspect the historic military structures of the Cu Chi Tunnels.
