Vietnam and Cambodia Discovery

This document is an eleven-day travel itinerary detailing a guided route through Vietnam and Cambodia. It maps out daily excursions across northern, central, and southern Vietnam before connecting travellers to Siem Reap.

Featured destinations include Hanoi, the karst landscapes of Ninh Binh, overnight cruising in Ha Long Bay, the historic streets and river of Hoi An, Ba Na Hills, and the river delta of Ben Tre. The guide outlines daily schedules, boat rides, cave visits, temple tours, transport arrangements, and scheduled meal plans.
