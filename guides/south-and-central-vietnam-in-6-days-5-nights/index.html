South And Central Vietnam in 6 Days 5 Nights

This itinerary guide provides a comprehensive six-day travel schedule through southern and central Vietnam for Indian visitors. It outlines day-to-day sightseeing programmes starting with early hotel check-in and historical exploration at the Cu Chi Tunnels, alongside river cruising and village cycling across the Mekong Delta.

The route moves to central Vietnam via a flight to Da Nang, featuring the Ba Na Hills cable car, the Golden Bridge, and indoor entertainment at Fantasy Park. Sightseeing concludes in Hoi An with traditional basket boat rowing in Cam Thanh Coconut Village, heritage walking tours around ancient merchant houses, and evening lantern releases on the river.
