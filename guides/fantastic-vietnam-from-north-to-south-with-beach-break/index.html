Fantastic Vietnam From North To South With Beach Break

This comprehensive itinerary guide outlines an eleven-day travel route from Hanoi to Ho Chi Minh City prepared by Vietnam Story for Indian travellers. It details daily schedules, guided excursions, hotel stays, and transport connections across northern, central, and southern Vietnam.

Featured destinations include Hanoi, Ninh Binh, Lan Ha Bay, Da Nang, Ba Na Hills, Nha Trang, and Ho Chi Minh City. Major activities covered in the programme range from touring Hoa Lu and the Cu Chi Tunnels to boating through Trang An, visiting the Golden Bridge, and taking mineral mud baths on Hon Tam Island.
