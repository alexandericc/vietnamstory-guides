Enchanting Center Package 4 Days 3 Nights

This publication outlines a four-day and three-night travel program covering Da Nang, Hoi An, Ba Na Hills, and Cham Island. It serves as an informative itinerary for visitors seeking a balanced look at the cultural landmarks and natural landscapes of Central Vietnam.

Documented stops include Non Nuoc Village, the historic Japanese Bridge Pagoda in Hoi An, the Golden Bridge and French Village atop Ba Na Hills, and Chong Beach on Cham Island. The plan also details airport transfers, English-speaking guides, scheduled meals, and included activities like snorkelling.
