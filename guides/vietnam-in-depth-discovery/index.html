Vietnam In depth Discovery

This comprehensive guide provides an eleven-day travel schedule across Vietnam tailored for Indian tourists visiting the country. It maps out daily activities, transport arrangements, and regional sightseeing from northern cultural centres to southern coastal retreats.

Featured destinations include Hanoi, the limestone landscapes of Ninh Binh, and overnight cruising in Ha Long Bay and Lan Ha Bay. Further stops feature Da Nang, the Golden Bridge at Ba Na Hills, Hoi An Ancient Town, Ho Chi Minh City, the waterways of Ben Tre in the Mekong Delta, and the beaches of Phu Quoc Island.
