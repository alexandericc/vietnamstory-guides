Spectacular Vietnam 8 Days 7 Nights With Phu Quoc Island

This eight-day travel guide from Vietnam Story provides an itemized itinerary through Hanoi, Ha Long Bay, Da Nang, Hoi An, and Phu Quoc Island for Indian travellers. It details daily sightseeing schedules, transportation modes, and guided excursions across each destination.
