6-Day Discovery Northern Splendor

This itinerary document serves as a comprehensive travel plan for a six-day journey across northern and central Vietnam. It outlines daily touring schedules starting in Hanoi, continuing through the limestone valleys of Ninh Binh, and sailing across Lan Ha Bay on an overnight vessel.
