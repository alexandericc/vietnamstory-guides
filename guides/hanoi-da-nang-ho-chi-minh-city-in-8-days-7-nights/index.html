Hanoi – Da Nang – Ho Chi Minh City in 8 Days 7 Nights

This guide details an eight-day travel route linking Hanoi, Da Nang, and Ho Chi Minh City through daily itineraries and logistics. It outlines scheduled morning and afternoon activities, accommodation stops, and transportation between Vietnam's northern, central, and southern regions.

Featured destinations include Hanoi's Temple of Literature and Hoa Lo Prison, boat excursions through Trang An and Lan Ha Bay, and the heritage streets of Hoi An. The guide also details excursions to Ba Na Hills and the Golden Bridge, as well as an exploration of the Cu Chi Tunnels near Ho Chi Minh City.
