Highlights of Vietnam and Cambodia Trip For Family

This comprehensive itinerary outlines a twelve-day family journey across key cultural and historical destinations in Vietnam and Cambodia.

The guide details scheduled tours through Hanoi, Ninh Binh, Halong Bay, Ho Chi Minh City, and the Mekong Delta before crossing into Cambodia to visit Phnom Penh and Siem Reap. Highlighted visits include the Temple of Literature, Cu Chi Tunnels, the Royal Palace, Tuol Sleng Genocide Museum, and the ancient temple complex of Angkor Wat.
